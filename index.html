Meet the artist

I'm Shreya.

I've been drawn to art for as long as I can remember. I grew up constantly drawing characters, imagining little stories and turning whatever was in my head into something I could put on paper. Somewhere along the way, I discovered animation, and I fell in love with the idea that those drawings didn't have to stay still.

Since then, I've spent years exploring illustration, character design and 2D animation — sharing little stories, everyday moments, jokes, feelings and pieces of imagination through my work online.

Eigaine Studios is my way of bringing all of that full circle: a space where the skills I've built through years of drawing and animating can become something bigger, and where I can help other people turn their ideas into visuals that feel alive. Whether it's a character, a story, a reel or an idea you've been carrying around for a while, I'd love to help bring it to life.

— Shreya
